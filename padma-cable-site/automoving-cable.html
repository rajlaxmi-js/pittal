Automoving Cable

Heat and abrasion resistant automotive wiring for two, three and four-wheelers.

Automoving Cable ISI Marked ISO 9001:2015

Under-bonnet and chassis wiring runs through constant vibration, heat cycling and tight bends. Our Automoving Cable range uses heat-stabilised insulation and fine-strand copper conductors to hold up across the vehicle's service life.

Technical Specification

ConductorFine-strand tinned copper, high flexibility
InsulationPVC / XLPE, heat & abrasion resistant
Temperature Rating-40°C to 105°C
Voltage GradeLow voltage automotive grade
Sizes0.5 sq.mm – 6 sq.mm
StandardIS / ARAI compliant

Applications

  • Two & three-wheeler wiring looms
  • Four-wheeler battery & harness cable
  • Chassis & engine bay wiring
  • Aftermarket automotive rewiring
