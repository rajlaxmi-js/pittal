About Padma Cable

33 years of manufacturing the wire this country runs on.

Founded in 1989 and headquartered in the Bawana Industrial Area of Delhi, Padma Cable Industries Pvt. Ltd. has grown from a single production line into one of the region's established names in PVC wire and cable manufacturing.

Our Story

Built in the copper and electrical industry, one certification at a time.

We take the honour of being a leading PVC wire and cable manufacturer, producing an extensive and diverse range of products for residential, industrial and automotive use. Since our inception, the "PADMA" brand has been shaped by consistent investment in product and system certification — not shortcuts.

1989

The Beginning

Padma Cable Industries is established in Delhi with a focus on PVC insulated wire manufacturing for the domestic market.

EARLY YEARS

Building Manufacturing Capability

Production capacity expands from household wire into industrial and power cable lines, backed by in-house quality testing.

CERTIFICATION

Standards & Accreditation

The PADMA brand is accredited with product and system certifications, formalising the quality processes behind every reel.

DIVERSIFICATION

Nine Product Lines

The range grows to cover power, control, data, communication and automotive cable — a full catalogue for industrial and OEM buyers.
